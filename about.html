Meerav Ki Kahani — Our Story

A small family kitchen in Bikaner, fried fresh every morning since 1983 — no shortcuts, no palm oil, no factory lines.

Four Decades of Aslee Bikaneri Taste
Meerav Ki Kahani

We still fry it the way Nani did.

Meerav began the way most honest kitchens do — not as a business plan, but as a family's own recipe box. In 1983, in a small kitchen in Bikaner, our Nani started frying namkeens the traditional way: hand-mixed spice blends, slow-fried batches, and a stubborn refusal to cut corners.

Four decades later, the kitchen has grown, but the method hasn't changed. We still measure our moth dal flour by hand, still test the oil temperature by feel, and still pack every order the same day it's fried — because that's the only way we know how to make it taste right.

No palm oil, no chemical preservatives, no factory shortcuts. Just pure cold-pressed groundnut oil, desert rock salt, and a family recipe that's never been written down — only passed on.

Our Vision

Aslee Bikaneri Swaad, Har Ghar Tak
